Privacy policy

Last updated September 20, 2026.

What you type into our tools is processed in your browser and is not sent to us. The tools do not store your entries. If you press “Copy link to this result”, your entries are placed in the copied link after the # sign so the person you share it with sees the same result; that part of a link is not sent to our server.

Analytics, advertising and partners

Analytics. This site does not currently run Google Analytics or any other visitor-tracking service. If that changes, this page will be updated first to name the service, what it collects and how to opt out.

What you type into the calculators. Your inputs, such as your state, electricity use and price, system size, loan terms and appliance list, are processed in your browser. They are not sent to this site or stored by it. They leave your browser only if you press “Copy link,” which creates a link containing your current inputs so you can return to them or share them. Anyone you send that link to can see those inputs.

Advertising and partners. At launch, this site shows no advertising and has no installer, lender, affiliate or lead-generation partners, and it does not sell personal information. Before any advertising network or partner is added, this policy will be updated to name it, describe what it collects and explain your choices.
