Where the numbers come from

The calculators here run on three named public data sources. Electricity prices are each state’s average residential rate from the U.S. Energy Information Administration’s Electric Power Monthly (currently June 2026), refreshed monthly. Installed cost per watt comes from EnergySage’s state cost pages (September 2026), also refreshed monthly. Yearly solar output per kW is modelled with the European Commission’s PVGIS 5.2 at each state capital, refreshed yearly. The federal credit status follows the IRS, and incentive research starts from DSIRE and each program’s own page. Every formula and assumption is listed on the methodology page. The site is maintained by Rooftop Payback, and all calculations run in your browser.
