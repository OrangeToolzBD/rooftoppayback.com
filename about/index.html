About Rooftop Payback

Rooftop Payback is a free solar payback calculator that uses EIA electricity prices, PVGIS solar production estimates and EnergySage installed-cost data, checked monthly.

Rooftop Payback is a set of free calculators for the numbers behind home solar: what a system costs in your state, how many panels you need, how long it takes to pay for itself, how a loan compares with your bill savings, and how to size a battery, a generator or a panel angle. The aim is to show the arithmetic in the open, with every default tied to a named public source and a date, and every default replaceable with your own figure.

The site is owned and maintained by Rooftop Payback. It is not a solar installer, lender or tax adviser, and it does not sell equipment or recommend installers. At launch it has no installer, lender or lead-generation partners. If that changes, the relationship will be disclosed in plain words on the pages it affects and in the privacy policy before it goes live.

Results are estimates, not quotes. Your installer’s proposal, your utility’s tariff and your own tax situation determine what applies to you. Electricity prices and installed costs are refreshed monthly and production estimates yearly, as described on the methodology page. Incentive and net metering details are added state by state only after they are checked on official pages and dated.

Corrections. If you find a wrong number, an outdated rule or a broken source link, please report it through the contact page, naming the page and the figure. Confirmed errors are corrected promptly, and the page’s updated date changes when they are.
