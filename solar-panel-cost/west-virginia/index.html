Solar Panel Cost in West Virginia

In West Virginia, a 7 kW home solar system costs about $20,300 before incentives at $2.90 per watt (EnergySage, September 2026). At 15.45¢ per kWh (EIA, June 2026), it pays for itself in about 14.8 years.
