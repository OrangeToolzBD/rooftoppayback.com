Solar Panel Cost in Arkansas

In Arkansas, a 7 kW home solar system costs about $16,730 before incentives at $2.39 per watt (EnergySage, September 2026). At 14.12¢ per kWh (EIA, June 2026), it pays for itself in about 11.7 years.
